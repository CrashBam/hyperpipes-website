Report a Bug

No account, no sign-up, nothing to log into. Fill in the boxes, press send, and it lands in the developer's inbox. It is one person reading these, so plain words are perfect — you do not need to know any technical terms.

In your own words. If you can say what you were doing just before it happened, that is the most useful part of all.

The single most useful thing you can give us — it recreates your exact track, dial for dial. Find it in the Race Lab, on a track's details, or on the finish screen after a Lab test race. Career and cup races have no code — just say which race it was in the box above, and leave this blank.
